Privacy policy

This policy covers Personal Tools, private utilities run locally by their owner. Depending on the tool and permissions granted, they access health and activity records, files, spreadsheets, and associated metadata solely for personal processing, backup, and synchronization. They may read, create, or update data in the owner's Google account.

Authorization credentials are stored locally. Data may be stored locally and in the owner's Google account. Connections to Google APIs use HTTPS to protect data in transit. The tools do not sell data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to other parties beyond the Google services used for these functions.

The owner controls retention and can delete local and Google-hosted copies separately and revoke access through their Google Account. Revoking access does not delete existing copies.

Use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.
